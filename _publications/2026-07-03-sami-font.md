Sami-Font generates a complete typeface from a single reference glyph. Style information is infused at multiple scales of the denoising network, while structure-aware losses preserve stroke topology during generation. The method is evaluated across Korean, Chinese, and Latin scripts and improves on prior one-shot and few-shot font generation baselines.
