This project explores diffusion models for generating high-quality Korean fonts. The goal is to produce structurally consistent and stylistically accurate glyph images learned from both printed and handwritten samples.

**Key contributions**

- Few-shot generation of unseen characters from 3–5 reference glyphs.
- Multi-scale style injection combined with CLIP-guided perceptual loss.
- Structural consistency enforced through a Sobel-edge loss.

[View the code on GitHub](https://github.com/abdulsami101/DK-Font) · Published at ICOIN 2025 and extended for journal submission.
