## Current role

**AI Engineer, DeltaX** <span class="date-inline">— Dec 2025 to present · Seoul, South Korea</span>

- Engineering end-to-end computer vision pipelines and post-processing logic for in-cabin monitoring systems (ICMS) in next-generation vehicles.
- Building driver and occupant monitoring features: seatbelt compliance, distraction detection (gaze away, phone use, hands off wheel), and occupant classification for adults, children, and pets.

## Selected work

**Vehicle perception and edge AI.** Real-time vision pipelines for occupant detection, 3D body keypoints, gaze tracking, and safety compliance. Hardware-aware optimization for constrained automotive silicon through post-training quantization with custom calibration, and end-to-end compilation along the PyTorch → ONNX → TI TIDL path for TDA4VM and AM6xA-class SoCs.

**Generative modelling and image synthesis.** Conditional diffusion models and GANs for high-fidelity generation and editing, image-to-image translation that preserves structural integrity across domains, and synthetic data pipelines built on foundation models such as SAM for inpainting, augmentation, and automated annotation.
